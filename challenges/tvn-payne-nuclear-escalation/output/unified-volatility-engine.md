## 3. Why the Same Structure Catches Both

### The Claim

The five invariant lenses (L3, L4, L5, L6, L7) fire on every Payne scenario because the detection primitives operate on **cognitive shape**, not **domain content**. A financial market crash and a nuclear escalation crisis produce different content but the same cognitive shape:

- **Gradient shift** (L1): Something changed from baseline
- **Resonance spike** (L3): The change is statistically significant
- **Co-occurrence** (L4): Multiple independent signals converged
- **Pattern match** (L5): The change maps to known failure vocabulary
- **State transition** (L6/L7): The change persists across observations

These are properties of the signal, not properties of the domain. Any domain where failure produces gradient shifts, resonance spikes, pattern matches, and persistent state transitions will be caught by the same engine.

## 4. The Design That Makes This Possible

### Archetype Space as Eigenvector Decomposition

The 14 archetypes are not categories — they are **eigenvectors of failure space**. Each archetype represents a basis function that failure modes project onto:

| Eigenvector | Failure Pattern | Financial Instance | Governance Instance |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cautionary_fall` | hubris → loss → moral | "We're too big to fail" → Lehman Brothers | "This opportunity may not come again" → Escalation dominance |
| `greed_backfire` | excess → punishment | Pump and dump → SEC enforcement | Resource extraction → regime collapse |
| `empire_cycle` | rise → apex → fall | Market bubble → crash → recovery | Hegemonic power → overextension → decline |
| `cunning_triumph` | wit overcomes strength | Spoofing/layering → profit | Wolf in sheep's clothing → deception gap |
| `catastrophe_rebuild` | destruction → recovery | Flash crash → circuit breaker → rebuild | Nuclear exchange → creative destruction → new order |
| `unity_strength` | cooperation → survival | Cartel coordination → price stability | Alliance credibility → mutual defense |

Any text expressing failure — in any domain — projects onto these eigenvectors. The decomposition is lossy (14 dimensions can't capture all nuance), but it's sufficient for detection because failure modes are lower-dimensional than they appear. A nuclear escalation crisis and a market crash look different on the surface but occupy the same region of archetype space.

### The Tricameral Memory Architecture

The detection primitives are calibrated against three timescales of human experience:

1. **Bedrock** (`civilization_priors`, 988 docs): Aesop's fables, The Prophet, world history milestones, sonar acoustics. These are the eigenvectors — patterns so fundamental they recur across millennia. Near-zero decay.

2. **Climate** (`tps_corpus`, 313 docs): The collective meaning-set of the current epoch. Current projections of contemporary events onto the eigenvectors. Epoch-bounded decay.

3. **Terrain** (`mogul_memory`, operational history): Recent operational data. Residuals after subtracting known basis vectors. High decay.

The harness operates primarily against Bedrock — the civilization priors are what make the detection domain-agnostic. "Golden egg" (Aesop) maps to `greed_backfire` whether the golden egg is a financial position or a nuclear deterrent. "Rise and fall" (world history) maps to `empire_cycle` whether the empire is a market leader or a geopolitical hegemon.

### The Gaussian Splat Primitive

The detection engine uses a shared mathematical primitive across all subsystems: `w = e^(-d²/2σ²)` — gaussian spatial decay with radius cutoff. This rewards neighborhood density, not just point similarity. Items in dense relevant clusters get boosted; isolated matches don't.

This is why the engine catches overlapping features: a text containing both financial and governance failure terms produces a denser cluster in archetype space than text containing terms from only one domain. The gaussian splat amplifies the convergence — the overlapping structure is literally what the math rewards.
